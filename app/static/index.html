How ClaimLens scores sources

The reading

For each source, a fine-tuned natural-language-inference model reads the page sentence by sentence and decides whether each sentence supports or opposes the claim. Those per-sentence judgments are aggregated into one stance per source, and the sources are combined into the overall reading shown above, weighted by how confident and how credible each one is. The bar position reflects that combined, confidence-weighted verdict, not a simple source count.

Source tiers

VerifiedFact-checkers and established encyclopedias or outlets with a known reliability record.
EstimatedAcademic, institutional, or topically relevant sources without a formal reliability rating.
UnverifiedOpen-web results with no established track record; shown for transparency, weighted least.

ClaimLens surfaces and weighs evidence from public sources; the reading reflects what those sources lean toward, not verified fact. Tiers and credibility scores are heuristic editorial estimates, not measured values. It is a research tool, not an arbiter of truth.
